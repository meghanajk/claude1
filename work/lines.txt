This Telugu man saved millions of lives, and almost nobody knows his name.
Yellapragada Subbarow.
He found how our body makes energy.
He helped create medicines that still fight cancer.
But his own life was full of pain.
Born poor. Lost his brothers to disease.
He sailed to America with almost no money.
Night job in a hospital. Day class at Harvard.
He gave the world answers. Harvard gave him no permanent job.
He kept working anyway.
He died at just 53.
The world forgot him. Don't.
